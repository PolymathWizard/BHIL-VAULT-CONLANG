## Attribution norms observed in the domain

- Screen conlangs credit both the franchise (IP owner) and the creator (author);
  the catalog's provenance block mirrors this dual attribution.
- Fan institutions that survive long-term (KLI, LearnNavi, Thuum) are the ones
  that name and honor the creator relationship explicitly.
- Course-exclusive and fan-coined vocabulary must never be attributed to the
  original creator; canonicity labeling exists to keep that line clean.
