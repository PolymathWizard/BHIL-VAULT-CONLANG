# Reading List and Reference Shelf

Curated entry points, grouped by depth. Items already in the catalog carry their asset id.

## Start here (the field in four books)

1. **The Art of Language Invention**, David J. Peterson, Penguin 2015 (d10).
   The practitioner's manual and the field's citation anchor.
2. **The Klingon Dictionary**, Marc Okrand, Pocket Books 1985/1992 (a01).
   The template every screen conlang followed.
3. **The Language Construction Kit**, Mark Rosenfelder (Zompist).
   The community's foundational how-to; companion to SCA2 (d04).
4. **In the Land of Invented Languages**, Arika Okrent, 2009.
   The accessible history of the whole enterprise, Esperanto to Klingon.

## Franchise deep dives

- **Tolkien:** Ardalambion's Quenya Course (d08); Eldamo (a03) for the data
  model; Parma Eldalamberon and Vinyar Tengwar for primary-source publication.
- **Star Trek:** The Klingon Way (1996) and Klingon for the Galactic Traveler
  (1997), both Okrand; HolQeD back issues via the KLI (b02).
- **Game of Thrones:** Living Language Dothraki (d09); Peterson's wiki (a06);
  the Duolingo High Valyrian course (b01).
- **Avatar:** naviteri.org (Frommer's living canon blog); the LearnNavi
  dictionary (a05).
- **Dune:** Peterson's glossed film dialogue at dedalvs.com; the Omniglot
  Chakobsa page (a09, c04).
- **The Elder Scrolls:** Thuum.org's print editions (a07), especially the
  Fifth Edition canonical dictionary.
- **The Expanse:** Nick Farmer's interviews and the SYFY Belter primer; the
  Fictionary compilation (a08).

## Scholarship and journals

- **Fiat Lingua** (b06): the monthly archive; start with the conlang-criticism
  and franchise-analysis pieces.
- **HolQeD** (ISSN 1061-2327): twelve years of Klingon linguistics.
- **Fauskanger's corpus analyses** (d08): the model for treating an invented
  language as a philological object.

## NLP and AI frontier

- Emergent Translation in Multi-Agent Communication, arXiv:1710.06922 (a11).
- ConlangCrafter, arXiv:2508.06094 (d11).
- ConlangBench, arXiv:2608.03505 (a10). Verification pending; read the flag in
  the catalog before citing.
- The Esperanto Universal Dependencies treebank, as the boundary marker for
  what no fiction conlang yet has.

## Tools to actually use

- PolyGlot (d01) for documentation; Vulgar (d02) for rapid generation;
  SCA2 (d04) and Lexurgy (d05) for diachrony; ConWorkShop (d03) for community
  and typology; boQwI' (d06) as the model of a living-canon dictionary app.
