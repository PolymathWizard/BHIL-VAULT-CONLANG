## Partnership patterns worth copying

1. **Creator plus community (LearnNavi model):** the creator retains canon
   authority and feeds new material through a named channel (naviteri.org)
   while the community runs infrastructure, tooling, and teaching.
2. **Institution plus platform (KLI plus Duolingo, KLI plus Microsoft):** the
   canon guardian supplies data quality; the platform supplies reach.
3. **Creator plus publisher (Peterson plus PRH/Penguin):** licensed,
   ISBN-anchored products that pay the creator and stabilize canon in print.

## Engagement etiquette

These are mostly volunteer institutions guarding franchise-adjacent material.
Approach with citation, attribution, and offers of tooling (metadata wrappers,
export formats) rather than extraction. The commercialization doc
(`docs/04-commercialization.md`) assumes this posture throughout.
