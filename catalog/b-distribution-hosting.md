# Category B: Distribution & Hosting

Nine assets. Publishers, platforms, reference hubs, and institutions acting as data services. Machine-readable form: `data/catalog.json` (ids `vault:conlang-b01` through `b09`).

---

## b01. Duolingo Conlang Courses `[S]`

- **URL:** https://www.duolingo.com/courses
- **Owner/Creator:** Duolingo, Inc. Course authors: David J. Peterson (High Valyrian, HBO partnership); Klingon Language Institute (Klingon).
- **Description:** The largest language-learning platform and the dominant conlang distribution channel. Carries High Valyrian (2019) and Klingon (launched 2018-03-15; now closed to new users but retained for current students). Esperanto sits at the auxlang boundary.
- **Metrics (all vendor self-reported, counters frozen since 2025-06-23, decayed):** High Valyrian roughly 1.06M total enrolled `[S]`; 514,000 active learners per Duolingo's 2022 blog (an active-learner figure, not total enrolled) `[S]`; Klingon roughly 297K learners `[S]`; 2022 House of the Dragon update added 159 words and five skills `[S]`; Klingon course wordsLearned roughly 1,533 per duome.eu `[U]`.
- **Rights flag:** franchise-ip (licensed course content on a proprietary platform)
- **Relations:** teaches High Valyrian and Klingon; partner edges to b02 and HBO.

## b02. Klingon Language Institute (kli.org) `[V]`

- **URL:** https://www.kli.org/
- **Owner/Creator:** Non-profit founded 1992 by Lawrence M. Schoen.
- **Description:** The canon-guarding institution for Klingon. Publishes the new-words ledger (a02), ran the HolQeD journal (1992-2004, ISSN 1061-2327), hosts the annual qep'a' conference with Okrand in attendance, runs the Klingon Language Certification Program, and publishes translated works.
- **Rights flag:** fair-use-community (operates with franchise awareness)
- **Relations:** guards_canon_of Klingon; partner to b01 and d07.

## b03. Omniglot `[V]`

- **URL:** https://www.omniglot.com/
- **Owner/Creator:** Simon Ager; launched 1998; ad, affiliate, and Patreon supported.
- **Description:** Encyclopedia of writing systems and languages; the central cross-franchise reference hub for constructed and fictional scripts, including the Chakobsa page.
- **Metrics:** 2,200+ language profiles; 371 writing systems; 1,137+ constructed, adapted, and fictional scripts `[S]` (site self-report, order corroborated by Wikipedia's "over 1,000").
- **Rights flag:** creator-owned
- **Relations:** hosts script references for c01 through c04.

## b04. LearnNavi.org `[C]`

- **URL:** https://learnnavi.org/
- **Owner/Creator:** Community organization, invited by Dr. Paul Frommer to co-develop Na'vi.
- **Description:** Hosts the authoritative Na'vi dictionary (a05), audio vocabulary, Discord lessons, and the Eana Eltu tooling. The best-practice model of a creator-community partnership.
- **Rights flag:** fair-use-community (canon: franchise-ip)
- **Relations:** hosts a05; teaches Na'vi; canon feed from naviteri.org.

## b05. Language Creation Society (LCS) `[V]`

- **URL:** https://conlang.org/
- **Owner/Creator:** Non-profit (San Diego, CA), founded 2007; historically led by Sai Emrys.
- **Description:** The professional body of conlanging. Ran the application process that staffed HBO's Dothraki search (30+ applicants, Peterson selected); operates Fiat Lingua, Lexember, the Language Creation Conference, a lending library, and member hosting.
- **Rights flag:** open-license (organizational content)
- **Relations:** hosts b06; professional network edge to every screen-conlang creator.

## b06. Fiat Lingua `[V]`

- **URL:** https://fiatlingua.org/
- **Owner/Creator:** Published by the LCS; monthly PDF archive since 2011.
- **Description:** The de facto journal of conlang scholarship: grammars, criticism, cognitive-linguistics applications, and franchise-conlang analysis, CC-licensed with formal citations.
- **Rights flag:** open-license
- **Relations:** cites the field's scholarship; hosted by b05.

## b07. Publisher Channels (Pocket Books / Simon & Schuster; Penguin Random House / Living Language) `[V]`

- **Description:** The licensed, ISBN-anchored commercial layer where franchise IP and conlang data formally intersect. Distributes a01 (The Klingon Dictionary), d09 (Living Language Dothraki, 2014), and d10 (The Art of Language Invention, 2015).
- **Rights flag:** franchise-ip (licensed products)
- **Relations:** hosts a01, d09, d10.

## b08. App Stores (Google Play / Apple App Store) `[S]`

- **Description:** Distribution channel for conlang apps, principally boQwI' (d06).
- **Metrics:** boQwI' 100K+ downloads, 4.4 stars across 749 reviews on Google Play `[S]` (store listing self-report).
- **Rights flag:** platform (per-app rights vary)
- **Relations:** hosts d06.

## b09. Fan Wikis (Fandom, Wiktionary appendices, Wikibooks, Linguifex) `[U]`

- **Description:** Distributed hosting of conlang lexicons and grammars: the Wiktionary Belter appendix, the Wikibooks Na'vi dictionary, the Fandom Dune and Expanse wikis, and Linguifex.
- **Metrics:** Linguifex carries 982 pages in its Languages category and 522 in Conlangs `[S]` (site self-report).
- **Rights flag:** fair-use-community
- **Data-quality note:** High reach, low provenance discipline. The fragmentation problem in microcosm, and the reason a cross-institution join key is on the recommendation list.
