# Category D: Technology & Tools

Eleven assets. Construction software, sound-change appliers, dictionary tooling, learning products, and NLP/MT projects. Machine-readable form: `data/catalog.json` (ids `vault:conlang-d01` through `d11`).

---

## d01. PolyGlot (Language Construction Kit) `[V]`

- **URL:** https://draquet.github.io/PolyGlot/ (source: https://github.com/DraqueT/PolyGlot)
- **Owner/Creator:** Draque Thompson. MIT license. Windows, macOS, Linux.
- **Description:** Desktop conlang toolkit: lexicon with advanced search, grammar guide, automated declension and conjugation, user-defined orthography, etymology trees, custom fonts, integrated Zompist word generator, recordable audio.
- **Rights flag:** open-license
- **Strategic value:** The leading free and open desktop documentation tool, and a natural export target for a standardized conlang format.

## d02. Vulgar (Vulgarlang) `[C]`

- **URL:** https://www.vulgarlang.com/
- **Owner/Creator:** "Linguistx." Freemium: demo capped at 200 words; paid one-time license lifts the cap to 4,000+.
- **Description:** One-click generator of phonology, grammar, and lexicon; advanced phonology includes a sound-change tool; the companion Vulgar Dictionaries service deploys searchable online conlang dictionaries.
- **Rights flag:** creator-owned (commercial)
- **Strategic value:** The dominant commercial generator; strong for rapid worldbuilding.

## d03. ConWorkShop (CWS) `[U]`

- **URL:** https://conworkshop.com/
- **Description:** Web-based conlang creation and community platform supporting online courses and cross-conlang statistics via CALS, the Conlang Atlas of Language Structures.
- **Rights flag:** fair-use-community
- **Strategic value:** The community and analytics layer; CALS is a rare structured typological dataset for conlangs.

## d04. Zompist SCA2 (Sound Change Applier 2) `[V]`

- **URL:** https://www.zompist.com/sca2.html
- **Owner/Creator:** Mark Rosenfelder, 2012. Browser-based, Unicode.
- **Description:** Applies ordered sound changes to a lexicon to derive descendant languages or reconstruct proto-forms; companion to the Zompist Language Construction Kit and the Gen word generator.
- **Rights flag:** open-license (freely provided)
- **Strategic value:** The canonical diachronic tool; the reference implementation the field measures others against.

## d05. Lexurgy `[C]`

- **URL:** https://www.lexurgy.com/sc
- **Owner/Creator:** Brad Neimann (bradrn).
- **Description:** A successor-class sound-change applier and word generator with multigraph support, suprasegmental handling, and daughter-language derivation. Active development.
- **Rights flag:** open-license
- **Strategic value:** The modern power-user SCA.

## d06. boQwI' (Klingon Language Assistant) `[C]`

- **URL:** Google Play / iOS (data: https://github.com/De7vID/klingon-assistant-data)
- **Owner/Creator:** Daniel L. Yonge-Mallo (De'vID). Open-source data repository.
- **Description:** Klingon lexicon, canon search, and grammar parser (not a general translator), updated within days of qep'a' word reveals (latest incorporated: qep'a' 32, 2025).
- **Metrics:** 100K+ downloads, 4.4 stars `[S]` (Play Store); advertised "3,000+ word" dictionary `[S]`; underlying aggregated lexicon 4,221 entries as of 2018-09 `[S]`.
- **Rights flag:** fair-use-community (canon: franchise-ip)
- **Strategic value:** Best-in-class living-canon dictionary app; the model for canon-tracked repositories.

## d07. Microsoft Bing Klingon Machine Translation `[V]`

- **URL:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/translator/blog/2013/05/14/announcing-klingon-for-bing-translator/
- **Owner/Creator:** Microsoft, announced 2013-05-14, built with the KLI (Lawrence Schoen), Marc Okrand, and Klingon-speaking Microsoft engineer Eric Andeen.
- **Description:** The first and still the only commercial-grade conlang machine-translation engine; supports Latin and pIqaD output.
- **Rights flag:** franchise-ip (licensed collaboration)
- **Relations:** derived_from a01/a02 corpus; pipeline reused in a11.

## d08. Ardalambion (+ Quenya Course) `[V]`

- **URL:** https://ardalambion.net/
- **Owner/Creator:** Helge Kåre Fauskanger; launched 1997.
- **Description:** The scholarly backbone of Elvish study: grammars, corpus analyses, the widely used Quenya Course, the Quettaparma Quenyallo wordlist, "Quest for Standard Sindarin," and a Neo-Quenya New Testament (2015).
- **Rights flag:** fair-use-community
- **Relations:** feeds a03 and a04; analyzes c05.

## d09. Living Language Dothraki `[V]`

- **URL:** Penguin Random House / Living Language, 2014-10-07 (ISBN 9780804160865)
- **Owner/Creator:** David J. Peterson. Franchise: HBO.
- **Description:** The official conversational Dothraki course: 128-page guide plus a one-hour audio CD, 500+ words and phrases including material coined exclusively for the course.
- **Rights flag:** franchise-ip (licensed product)
- **Strategic value:** The canonical licensed print-and-audio learning product for a screen conlang; a direct commercial precedent for the GamingToolset line.

## d10. The Art of Language Invention `[V]`

- **URL:** https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/316207/ (companion: artoflanguageinvention.com)
- **Owner/Creator:** David J. Peterson; Penguin, 2015 (ISBN 9780143126461).
- **Description:** The definitive practitioner's manual for conlang construction, from Tolkien and Klingon through the modern conlanging community, with an accompanying online course and video series.
- **Rights flag:** creator-owned (published)
- **Strategic value:** The field's canonical methodology reference and citation anchor for construction technique.

## d11. ConlangCrafter (LLM conlang generation) `[V]`

- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.06094 (project: conlangcrafter.github.io)
- **Owner/Creator:** Alper, Yanuka, Giryes (Tel Aviv University) and Beguš (UC Berkeley).
- **Description:** Multi-stage LLM pipeline generating internally consistent, typologically diverse conlangs (phonology, then morphology, then syntax), treating LLM confabulation as a creative feature.
- **Rights flag:** open-license (paper)
- **Strategic value:** State of the art in AI conlang generation; the emerging AI-tool frontier for the domain.
