# Category C: Visualizations & Schematics

Five assets. Canonical scripts, charts, and diagrams. Machine-readable form: `data/catalog.json` (ids `vault:conlang-c01` through `c05`).

---

## c01. Tengwar & Cirth (Tolkien's Scripts) `[V]`

- **Owner/Creator:** J.R.R. Tolkien (creator-designed). Indexed by Mellonath Daeron's DTS/DCS specimina catalogs.
- **Description:** Tolkien's own writing systems: the Tengwar (Feanorian letters) and the Cirth runes, with canonical charts published in The Lord of the Rings appendices and later scholarship.
- **Rights flag:** franchise-ip (Tolkien Estate)
- **Strategic value:** The most-reproduced conlang visualization set; the core of any script-chart product.

## c02. Klingon pIqaD `[C]`

- **Owner/Creator:** Franchise design (Paramount/CBS); systematized by the KLI community.
- **Description:** The Klingon writing system. Used decoratively on screen (Latin transliteration is the working orthography); supported digitally in Bing Translator output and dCode tooling.
- **Rights flag:** franchise-ip
- **Relations:** rendered by d07; documented via b02.

## c03. Dovahzul Runes `[C]`

- **Owner/Creator:** Bethesda Game Studios (in-game Word Walls); per-word rune spellings in the Thuum.org Fourth Edition dictionary; fan fonts exist (Kazmirus).
- **Description:** The dragon runic script of Skyrim, carved as Word Walls in-game; three-character name and shout structure.
- **Rights flag:** franchise-ip (fonts: fair-use-community)
- **Relations:** documents Dovahzul; sourced from a07.

## c04. Chakobsa Abugida (Dune films) `[C]`

- **Owner/Creator:** David J. and Jessie Peterson for the 2021/2024 films; documented on Omniglot with a typeable font.
- **Description:** Left-to-right abugida designed for the films; consonant glyphs change shape by attached vowel. The newest canonical constructed script with an official font.
- **Rights flag:** franchise-ip (Legendary)
- **Relations:** script of a09; hosted by b03.

## c05. Tolkien Language Family Trees `[V]`

- **Owner/Creator:** J.R.R. Tolkien (original diagrams); reflected in Eldamo's period model and Fauskanger's "Evolution from Primitive Elvish to Quenya."
- **Description:** Tolkien's own diagrams of Elvish descent (Primitive Elvish to Common Eldarin to the Quenya, Telerin, and Sindarin lineages). The canonical derived_from visualization and a template for diagramming conlang genealogies generally.
- **Rights flag:** franchise-ip (Tolkien Estate; scholarly reproductions fair-use)
- **Relations:** modeled by a03; analyzed by d08.
